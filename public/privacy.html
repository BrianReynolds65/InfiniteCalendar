Privacy Policy

Infinite Calendar is a privately operated calendar and task-list interface intended for use by the site operator and people they authorize. This policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

Information the app accesses

If you choose to connect a Google Account, the app requests permission to list your Google Calendars, read and create, edit, or delete calendar events, and read and manage Google Tasks and task lists. It uses this information to show the calendars and task lists you select and to perform actions you request.

The app does not access Google data until you authorize it. You can choose which calendars and task lists to display.

How information is used and stored

Google Calendar and Tasks data is used to provide the app's calendar and task features. The app does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it for purposes unrelated to those features.

The app's server stores OAuth tokens needed to maintain your Google connection and your selected calendar and task-list IDs. These files are encrypted using ASP.NET Core Data Protection and stored on the privately operated server. The browser uses protected, HTTP-only cookies to maintain the app session and settings. Calendar events and task content are requested from Google when needed and are not intended to be kept as a separate, persistent copy by the app.

The app's operator is responsible for controlling access to the server, its network, backups, and stored data. Anyone who can access the server or its backups may be able to access information held there. If backups are made, they may retain stored data until those backups are rotated or deleted.

Sharing

The app does not sell or share Google user data with advertisers. Google data is sent to Google as needed to authorize the connection and provide the Google Calendar and Tasks features. The app operator may disclose information if required by law or to protect the security of the service.

Retention and your choices

Your Google connection and saved selections remain on the server until you disconnect the account or the operator removes the stored data. Use the app's Disconnect Google setting to remove the app's stored Google connection and selections. You can also revoke the app's access in your Google Account security settings.

Disconnecting or revoking access does not delete information already held by Google. Calendar events and tasks remain subject to your Google Account settings and Google's privacy practices.

Security

The app encrypts stored OAuth token and selection files and uses HTTPS when deployed as configured. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure; the operator is responsible for securing the server and its backups.

Children

This privately operated app is not directed to children and is not intended for independent use by children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. For privacy questions or requests concerning information stored by this app, contact brianreynolds65@gmail.com.
